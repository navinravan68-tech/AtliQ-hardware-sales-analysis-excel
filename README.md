# AtliQ Hardware Sales Analysis using Excel

## Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive sales and financial performance analysis for AtliQ Hardware. The objective was to transform raw business data into actionable insights using Microsoft Excel's advanced analytics capabilities, including Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, and Pivot Tables.

The analysis focuses on market performance, customer sales growth, profit and loss trends, and top-performing products to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Business Problem

AtliQ Hardware operates across multiple markets and customer segments. Management requires a consolidated view of:

* Market-wise sales performance
* Customer revenue contribution
* Profitability trends across fiscal periods
* Product performance and growth
* Comparison of actual sales against targets

The goal is to identify growth opportunities, high-performing markets, and areas requiring improvement.

## Reports Included

### 1. Market Performance Report

Analyzed sales performance across global markets and compared actual sales against predefined targets.

### 2. Customer Net Sales Report

Evaluated customer-wise net sales and year-over-year growth performance.

### 3. Profit & Loss by Fiscal Month

Tracked monthly revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross margin, and profitability trends.

### 4. Profit & Loss by Fiscal Year

Provided a yearly financial performance summary including Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and Gross Margin Percentage.

### 5. Top 10 Products Report

Identified the highest-performing products based on sales revenue and growth rate.

---

## Key Business Insights

### Revenue Growth

* Net Sales increased from **$196.7M in FY2020** to **$598.9M in FY2021**.
* Overall sales growth reached **204.5% year-over-year**.

### Market Performance

* India emerged as the highest revenue-generating market.
* Several international markets demonstrated strong year-over-year growth despite missing target values.

### Customer Analysis

* Amazon was among the top revenue-generating customers.
* Significant customer growth was observed across multiple retail and e-commerce channels.

### Profitability Analysis

* Gross Margin increased substantially alongside revenue growth.
* Profitability remained strong despite increased operational scale.

### Product Performance

* AQ Electron 4 3600 Desktop Processor was the highest-performing product.
* Multiple products achieved exceptional growth compared to the previous fiscal year.
